Open Source · MIT · Template

Build companion tools on top of any web-based Loan Origination System

LOS Companion is a free Chrome Extension template for developers whose LOS is a closed system by design — no public API, no interconnectivity, no vendor approval process. Copy the template, point it at your LOS, and start building the integrations your vendor never shipped.

Built for closed LOS platforms

No API? No problem.

Works with LOS platforms that have little or no interconnectivity. The extension reads what is already in the page — URL structure and localStorage — and injects its own UI. No vendor keys, no integration approvals.

No approval needed.

The secondary use case: personal or internal tools where you would never get permission to build against the LOS directly. The overlay UI is isolated in a Shadow DOM and talks to your own backend — not the vendor's.

Any platform, one config.

Zero hardcoded LOS specifics. Add your hostnames and a URL pattern with named capture groups — every component (context extraction, overlays, side panel, telemetry) keys off that single configuration.

Companion apps, minus the friction.

SPA-aware route tracking, a reactive side panel, presence heartbeats for time tracking, and a background message bus ready for your fetch() calls to CRMs, webhooks, and databases.

A starting point, not a finished product

This is a template to copy and build on — not an app you download and use today. It gives you the foundation: the parts that are annoying, fragile, and tedious to get right against a hostile SPA. What you build on top is yours to decide: CRM sync, video rooms, checklists, dashboards, custom workflows.

Clone or copy the repo. It is plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no build step, no framework, no dependencies to audit.

Configure two things in config/los-config.js and manifest.json: your LOS's domains, and its URL pattern for extracting the active record ID and tab.

Load it unpacked in Chrome and confirm context extraction against your LOS. The side panel shows you exactly what the template sees.

Build your features. Wire the declarative button config, the background message bus, and the data egress stub to whatever your workflow needs.
